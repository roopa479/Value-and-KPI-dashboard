Efficiency arrived in 2023, not through digital adoption

Overhead Ratio fell from 59.2% in 2022 to 51.7% in 2024 after net revenue jumped 23% in 2023. Active Digital Customers grew 5% to 7% every year with no step change.

Technology intensity did not run ahead of the business

Technology Expense as % of Net Revenue stayed between 9.6% and 11.1% in every disclosed year, peaking at 11.1% in 2022 before revenue outgrew spending.

Branches rebounded and the satisfaction gap narrowed

Chase Branch Count rose from a 4,787 trough in 2022 to 5,083 in 2025, while the J.D. Power Satisfaction Gap vs. Study Average narrowed from 30 to 11 points.

Bottom line: resilience proven, leverage not yet. Three tests of the thesis, read from the charts below:

Context that affects how to read these charts

  • Rate cycle. Net interest income makes revenue rate-sensitive. Net revenue rose 23% in 2023, so the Overhead Ratio and Net Revenue per Employee partly reflect interest rates, not only technology.
  • First Republic (May 2023). The acquisition inflates 2023 headcount, branches and revenue.
  • Visa share gain (2024). A $7.9B gain lifted 2024 reported net revenue.
  • Technology expense basis. The series is the firm's own total technology expense. Earlier "tech budget" figures use a narrower definition and are not comparable. Investor-day decks differ slightly for 2022 to 2023 ($14.1B vs $14.3B; $15.3B vs $15.5B); this dashboard uses the latest actuals basis.
  • J.D. Power study. J.D. Power redefined "national bank" over time ($200B deposit threshold in 2022, $250B in 2025); the 2024 study average is derived from the 2025 release.
  • 2026 outlook. The firm plans $19.8B of technology investment in 2026 (reference only, not charted).

Estimation and calculation notes

  • 2025 Total Technology Expense ($17.9B) is an estimate. It is management's technology expense outlook, not a reported actual. The 2025 Technology Expense as % of Net Revenue (9.8% est.) is calculated from it. Both are labeled "est." and should be replaced once the 2025 annual report figure is confirmed.
  • Calculated series: Technology Expense as % of Net Revenue, Mobile Share of Digital Customers, Net Revenue per Employee and the J.D. Power Satisfaction Gap are calculated from disclosed inputs; formulas are shown in the source table.
  • Not disclosed: Total Technology Expense for 2017, 2018 and 2020 is not disclosed on a consistent basis (n/d), so the technology chart starts in 2019 and its line spans the 2020 gap with a dashed segment.
  • Window: all series cover every year available from 2017 (or the first disclosed year) through 2025. Unfavorable years are kept in the data.
